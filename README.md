China’s external surplus, household demand, and local fiscal pressure should be considered together. A durable adjustment combines stronger household income and social protection with stable local revenue and reliable funding for public services. The NPC approves national plans, laws and budget authority; the State Council and ministries usually design specific programs within that authority.

中国的对外顺差、居民需求与地方财政压力应当放在一起分析。长期调整需要同时提高居民收入和社会保障水平、建立稳定的地方收入体系，并保障公共服务的可持续资金来源。全国人大及其常委会承担国家规划、法律与预算授权等职责；国务院和各部委通常在相应权限内制定具体政策。
