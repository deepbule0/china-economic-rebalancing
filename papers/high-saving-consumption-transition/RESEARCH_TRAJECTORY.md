# Development of the argument / 核心知识轨迹

This note records the logic of the research questions rather than treating earlier conversational assertions as evidence. / 以下整理问题的演进；早先对话本身不是事实依据。

| Step / 阶段 | Question / 问题 | What changes in the argument / 论证推进 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Is high saving simply cultural? / 高储蓄是否由文化决定？ | Culture may matter, but institutional and historical variation makes a deterministic account inadequate. / 承认文化可能作用，否定其足以独立解释一切。 |
| 2 | Is insufficient welfare the central cause? / 是否主要因为福利不足？ | Separate risk pooling from compulsory individual accumulation; Singapore is a crucial institutional comparison. / 区分共担风险和强制积累，不能用福利标签推断储蓄结果。 |
| 3 | Why can the United States save little? / 美国为何低储蓄？ | Add public transfers, balance sheets, government dissaving, and third-party expenditure; avoid attributing everything to household debt. / 纳入政府账户与统计构成。 |
| 4 | Who saves? / 谁在储蓄？ | Separate households, nonfinancial firms, financial firms, and government. Gross/net and denominator consistency become prerequisites. / 部门分解先于机制归因。 |
| 5 | Is low household consumption an income-share or propensity problem? / 收入份额还是储蓄倾向？ | Both enter an accounting identity; neither causal weight is established by the identity. Chinese income shares recovered after earlier decline. / 两条渠道都存在，须由数据判断其权重。 |
| 6 | Does corporate saving imply waste? / 企业储蓄是否等于浪费？ | Distinguish depreciation, productive investment, cash stocks, net lending, ownership, and social returns. / 评价边际配置和收益归属，不能用总量直接判低效。 |
| 7 | Why is transformation difficult? / 转型难在哪里？ | The joint hypothesis concerns income transmission, household risk, capital returns, and central-local fiscal responsibilities. / 形成部门与财政制度的联合机制。 |
| 8 | Can every country adopt an export-surplus model? / 能否各国都靠顺差？ | Global balances constrain common strategies; adjustment has to be coordinated. / 全球加总约束决定不能简单复制。 |
| 9 | What is the ultimate objective? / 最终目标是什么？ | Risk-adjusted welfare, sustainable services, opportunity, and ecological/fiscal resilience, rather than maximizing a single ratio. / 以长期居民福利评价积累和消费。 |

**Central thesis / 中心论点：** Institutions that distribute productive returns, share household risks, and govern capital use may lag the growth of productive capacity. The paper makes this a testable proposition, not a proven universal law. / 生产能力、收入分配、风险保障和资本治理需要同步演进；这一命题需要实证检验。

**Evidence still needed / 后续证据：** Same-year sector accounts for all economies; distributional income and consumption; policy exposure with credible comparison groups; marginal investment returns and consolidated public net assets; service quality and long-term liabilities. / 部门统一核算、收入分配、微观政策识别、边际投资收益、公共净资产与服务质量。
